## The 45-minute clock

`course/week-01/README.md` sets a hard stop. Split it:

- 15 minutes: `03-treasury-fund-mechanics.md`, because the asset comes first;
- 15 minutes: `01-token-standards.md`;
- 10 minutes: `02-sec-tokenized-securities.md`, intro and taxonomy only;
- 5 minutes: re-skim `../../../CONTEXT.md` and mark every term you could not
  have defined before today.

Stop at 45 minutes even mid-sentence. Unfinished reading goes in the carryover
line of the tracker, not into overtime.

## Out of scope this week

Solidity, ERC-4626, ERC-3643, ERC-7540, gas, wallets, and any live protocol.
The syllabus reaches those from Week 17 onward. Chasing them today costs you
the Week 1 gate.

## Rule for using these notes

Each file cites its sources. Before you repeat a claim in your own notes or in
a public post, open the citation and confirm the source says it. The reading
plan calls a walkthrough "not an audit" for the same reason.
